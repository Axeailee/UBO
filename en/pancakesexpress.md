# Quick Pancakes 🥞
***
## Ingredients (for 2 people):
- 1 small jar of applesauce (any flavor will do)
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons of flour
- 1/4 packet of baking powder
- A pinch of salt
- A small knob of butter for cooking
- (Optional) Chocolate chips or dried fruit
- It contains no ~~fat~~  

![alt text](https://4.bp.blogspot.com/-fCIz53Vlo4E/UMz6ZWIYAeI/AAAAAAAACTQ/28G4tZmfw2g/s1600/157+quinoa+pancakes.jpg)

***
## Preparation:

1. In a bowl, pour in the small jar of applesauce, crack an egg, and mix.
2. Then add the flour, baking powder, salt, and chocolate chips or dried fruit if desired.
3. Mix all the ingredients until you get a smooth batter.
4. Melt a small knob of butter in a pan and pour half of the mixture into two small dollops.
5. Flip the pancakes after __2–3 minutes__ over medium heat and let them puff up for another __1 minute__.
6. Repeat the process to make 2 more pancakes.

Enjoy your meal!
