# Mushroom Risotto 🍄‍🟫
***
## Ingredients (for 2 people):
- 120 g Arborio rice or round rice
- 2 onions
- 100 g mushrooms
- 500 mL boiling water for the vegetable stock cube
- 100 g grated Parmesan
- A small knob of butter
- A drizzle of olive oil

![alt text](https://assets.afcdn.com/recipe/20221108/137260_w1024h1024c1cx1048cy721cxt0cyt0cxb2119cyb1414.jpg)

***
## Preparation:

1. Peel and finely slice the onions.
2. In a pan, sauté the onions over medium heat with a drizzle of olive oil and a knob of butter.
3. Once they turn a nice golden color, pour in half of the previously prepared stock.
4. When the onions are cooked, add the rice and the remaining stock, then cover the pan and cook for 10 minutes.
5. Add the grated Parmesan and the washed, sliced mushrooms.
6. Continue cooking, stirring regularly, until all the liquid has evaporated.

> Enjoy your meal!
